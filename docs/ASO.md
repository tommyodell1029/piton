# App Store Optimization — Draft Listing

This is the baseline the `aso-agent` reads and iterates on once real

## Positioning

Piton is not another habit tracker. Most rely on the honor system — you tap
a checkbox and move on. Piton requires proof: a photo, your location,
HealthKit/Health Connect data, a timer, or an AI-reviewed image.

**Slogan:** Don't check it off. Prove it.

## App Store (Apple)

- **Title (≤30 chars):** `Piton: Prove Your Habits`
- **Subtitle (≤30 chars):** `Habit tracker with real proof`
- **Keyword field (≤100 chars):** `habit,streak,accountability,fitness,proof,tracker,gym,discipline,goals,routine`
- **Promotional text:** Don't check it off. Prove it. Verify every habit with
  photo, GPS, HealthKit, or AI — real accountability, real streaks.

## Play Store (Google)

- **Title (≤30 chars):** `Piton - Prove Your Habits`
- **Short description (≤80 chars):** `Habit tracker that requires proof — photo, GPS, health data, or AI review.`
- **Long description:** (draft — see agent report for the iterated version)
  Most habit apps run on the honor system: tap a checkbox, feel good, quit
  in two weeks. Piton is different. Every habit you build gets verified —
  with a photo, your location, Health Connect data, a timer, or an
  AI-reviewed image — so your streak means something.

  Build real habits with real proof:
  - Fitness: verify gym visits with photos, GPS, or workout data
  - Reading: prove your reading time with a timer or photo
  - Meditation: verify with a timer or photo
  - Custom habits: AI-powered proof for anything you want to build

  Stay accountable with friends, join challenges, and climb the leaderboard.
  Level up, earn badges, and get coached by an AI that actually knows your
  habits and streaks.

  Don't check it off. Prove it.
