Piton Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Piton ("we," "us," "our") is a habit-tracking app that verifies habit completion using proof — photos, location, health data, timers, and AI-reviewed images. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, and if you use Apple or Google Sign-In, the name/email they share with us.
  • Habit and verification data: the habits you create, your streaks, and the proof you submit to verify them (photos, timer durations, GPS coordinates, health metrics).
  • Photos: images you submit as proof are stored securely and may be analyzed by an AI model to confirm they match the habit you're verifying. We do not use your photos for any purpose beyond that verification.
  • Location: collected only when you submit a location-based proof, to confirm you were where the habit requires (e.g. the gym). We do not track your location in the background.
  • Health data: with your explicit permission, we read step count, active energy, and exercise minutes from Apple Health or Health Connect to verify fitness-related habits. We never write to your health records, and health data is used only for verification.
  • Subscription and payment information: if you subscribe to Piton Premium, payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google's in-app purchase system and our payments provider (RevenueCat) — we never see or store your card details.
  • Push notification identifiers: a device token used to deliver habit reminders, managed via OneSignal.
  • Usage analytics: basic app usage events (e.g. habit created, proof submitted) to understand what's working and fix bugs.

How we use your information

  • To operate the core product: creating habits, verifying proof, calculating streaks, and awarding badges/XP.
  • To power the AI Coach and AI photo-verification features.
  • To process subscription payments and manage your entitlements.
  • To send habit reminders and important account notifications.
  • To improve the app based on aggregate usage patterns.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers who help us run Piton, each bound to use it solely to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase — our database, authentication, and file storage provider.
  • Anthropic / OpenAI — process habit context and submitted photos to power the AI Coach and AI photo verification.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription status; payment itself is processed by Apple/Google.
  • OneSignal — delivers push notifications.
  • Apple and Google, as required to operate Sign in with Apple/Google and in-app purchases.

If you share proof, streaks, or achievements with friends inside the app (leaderboards, groups, challenges), that information is visible to the friends/group you choose to share it with.

Children's privacy

Piton is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • You can revoke camera, location, health, or notification permissions at any time in your device settings — Piton will simply be unable to verify the habit types that need them.
  • You can delete individual habits and proof from within the app.
  • You can request full account deletion as described above.
